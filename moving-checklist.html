Your First 48 Hours After Moving: A Practical Checklist

Avoid opening ten boxes just to find a phone charger. This free checklist helps you pack one essentials bag, label boxes so you can locate them, and decide what to unpack first.

Independent practical guide · Updated October 2026 · No sign-up required

1. Pack one first-night bag

  • Phone chargers and power strip
  • Toiletries, towels and toilet paper
  • Two days of clothing and bedding
  • Basic kitchen supplies, water and snacks
  • Important personal documents kept securely with you
  • Any essentials specific to your household

2. Number every box

Use a unique box ID such as K-01 for kitchen box 1 or B-02 for bedroom box 2. Write the destination room and a short contents summary on two sides of the box. Mark the boxes needed on day one with a visible star.

Example box index

K-01 — Kitchen — mugs, kettle, dish soap — unpack first: yes
B-02 — Bedroom — spare sheets, pillows — unpack first: yes
L-03 — Living room — books, frames — unpack first: no

Do not write sensitive personal information on external box labels.

3. The moving-day order

  1. Confirm all boxes arrived and note missing IDs.
  2. Set up a safe sleeping space and basic lighting.
  3. Locate the first-night bag and essential chargers.
  4. Unpack bathroom and kitchen essentials.
  5. Leave nonessential decorations and books for later.

4. Morning-after check

  • Check whether all essential boxes are accounted for.
  • Make a short list of items you cannot locate.
  • Prioritize only the next 3–5 boxes to unpack.
  • Keep packing materials organized for disposal or reuse.
